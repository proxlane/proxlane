Why route at all

No provider works on every target.

Teams end up with two or three accounts and a pile of glue code that switches between them. Proxlane is that glue code, extracted and made honest.

Failover between providers

A provider starts failing at 2am and the request finishes elsewhere. No provider will ever fail over to a competitor. We will.

Success you can trust

Providers return 200 with a captcha page in the body. Every response goes through block detection, and you see which rule fired.

Cost across providers

Your provider's dashboard shows their numbers. Proxlane shows which provider wins on a given domain, and what each one costs you there.
